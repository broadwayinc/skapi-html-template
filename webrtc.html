RTC Video Chat

WebRTC connects two browsers directly for video, audio, the screen and data. Skapi does the signaling over its realtime connection: skapi.connectRTC() calls another user, the callee receives an rtc:incoming message and answers with connectRTC() on it, and Skapi negotiates the peer connection between the two.

The media option names what to send: the camera, the microphone, the screen, or any mix. During a call, share() and unshare() add and remove the screen, and mute() pauses the microphone or the camera. The other side tells the tracks apart by the source of its track events.

Users find each other in the realtime group RTCCall. Open this page in another browser or device with a different account and call each other. WebRTC works over HTTPS, or on localhost.
